# FocusLoop

> Give your goals a structure. Give your ideas a place. Give your priorities your time.

FocusLoop is a personal execution coach that helps turn goals into consistent daily execution. Instead of managing endless task lists, it helps bridge the gap between **what you want to achieve** and **what you actually spend your time doing**.

---

## The Problem

Most of us don't have just one goal—we have dozens.

Career growth. AI projects. Personal branding. Podcasts. Networking. Mentoring. Writing. Learning.

The challenge isn't capturing ideas; it's turning them into meaningful progress.

Goals stay vague, ideas live across notes and documents, and our days get consumed by whatever feels urgent instead of what is important.

Existing productivity tools are excellent at managing **tasks** and **calendars**. FocusLoop is designed to manage **commitments**.

It provides one place where goals, ideas, actions and time commitments come together.

---

## The Product Vision

FocusLoop gives structure to goals by breaking them into smaller **Actions** and helping users commit dedicated time to those Actions.

The core philosophy is:

> Don't help me manage more tasks. Help me do what I committed to.

The product connects long-term intentions with everyday execution.

### FocusLoop Framework

Month → Goal → Action → Weekly Commitment → Today's Focus → Review

- **Month** — What bigger outcomes am I working toward?
- **Goal** — What meaningful outcome do I want to achieve?
- **Action** — What concrete work moves this Goal forward?
- **Weekly Commitment** — What am I committing to move this week?
- **Today** — What am I committing time to today?
- **Review** — Did my time and output match my commitments?

## Example

### Goal

**Build my personal brand**

### Actions

- Build Topmate profile
- Update product portfolio
- Publish LinkedIn posts
- Write Substack articles
- Identify speaking opportunities

### Weekly Commitment

- Publish 2 LinkedIn posts.
- Finish portfolio update.
- Draft one Substack article.

### Today's Focus

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 9:00–10:30 | Apply to targeted job openings |
| 11:00–12:00 | Build professional connections |
| 1:30–3:00 | Build FocusLoop |
| 4:00–5:00 | Draft LinkedIn post |

## MVP Scope (Version 1)

Current MVP includes:

- Monthly Goals
- Weekly Commitments
- Daily Focus Blocks
- Goal → Action relationship
- Start / Complete / Reschedule interactions
- Lightweight content planning for LinkedIn and Substack
- Weekly Review
- Local browser persistence using localStorage

### Deliberately Not Included Yet

- Authentication
- Database
- AI recommendations
- Google Calendar integration
- LinkedIn/Substack integration
- Automatic activity monitoring
- Voice assistant

The goal is to validate the core accountability loop before adding complexity.

---

## Product Principles

FocusLoop is intentionally **not**:

- A to-do list.
- A project management tool.
- A calendar replacement.
- A dashboard full of productivity metrics.

It is a personal execution system designed around one question:

> **What did I commit to, and did I actually do it?**
